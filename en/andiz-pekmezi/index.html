Weight350 gOriginTürkiye

Andız Pekmez: A Traditional Taste with a Resinous Aroma

Andız Pekmez is a traditional, dark-colored pekmez with a distinctive aroma, made from the berries of the andız (Syrian juniper) tree. While sweet, it carries slightly resinous and woody notes; in this respect it is easily distinguished from fruit pekmez. It may seem unusual at first taste, but for its fans this character is the product's most defining feature.

Andız pekmez is one of the traditional flavors passed down from generation to generation in Türkiye, yet less known today. By adding this product to the pekmez varieties it offers from Gürün/Sivas, Tufan Organik brings it to those curious about old flavors. The 350-gram jar is suitable for getting to know the product and using it in moderation at the breakfast table.

Serving Suggestions

  • Spread a thin layer over slices of bread topped with clotted cream.
  • Drizzle a dessert spoonful over plain yogurt and serve with walnut kernels.
  • Stir a spoonful into lukewarm water and serve as a traditional drink at teatime.

Size: 350 g
