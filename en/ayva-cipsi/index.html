Quince Chips: The Astringent Aroma of Quince in Crisp Form

Quince is easy to tell apart from other fruits thanks to its distinctive fragrance and slightly astringent taste. Prepared as chips, this traditional product turns the quince's firm flesh into thin, crisp slices. The flavour sits between sweet and tangy; the slight astringency it leaves on the palate will feel familiar to anyone who loves quince dessert or quince jam.

In Turkish cuisine, quince is one of the favourite fruits of the winter months. Tillo Kochari's quince chips bring this flavour from Gaziantep to your table whatever the season. They can be enjoyed on their own with tea, and they also add character to dessert and salad plates. Even a small amount carries the familiar scent of quince into the bowl.

Serving Suggestions

  • Set a few slices beside a quince dessert with clotted cream to add a different texture.
  • Serve them in a small bowl with strong black tea instead of sugar.
  • Chop them and scatter over winter salads with pomegranate seeds and walnuts.
