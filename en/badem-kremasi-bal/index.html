Tillo Kochari Almond Cream (Honey) is an almond cream sweetened with honey from the Almond Butters & Creams range, offered in 300 g as a natural food from Gaziantep.

Weight300 gOriginTürkiye

Almond Cream (Honey): The Gentle Harmony of Almonds and Honey

In this cream, the almonds are sweetened with honey. The sweetness of honey complements the soft aroma of almonds without masking it, giving the cream a rounded, balanced taste. Among Tillo Kochari's Gaziantep-rooted jars, it brings the two flavors together for those who would rather not choose between honey and almond cream at breakfast.

Since it contains honey, additional sweetening is usually unnecessary; spreading it on bread or enjoying a spoonful alongside tea is enough. At breakfasts that bring cheese and sweets together on the same plate, it strikes a pleasant balance, especially with white cheese and walnuts. The 300 g jar is a handy size for family breakfasts.

Serving Suggestions

  • Serve it beside slices of white cheese together with walnut halves.
  • Spread it on toasted bread and top with thinly sliced figs.
  • Layer it with plain yogurt and granola to make a breakfast bowl.

Weight: 300 g
