Apple Chips: Crisp Apple in Thin Slices

Apple chips are the crisp form of thinly sliced apple. The apple's natural shape is easy to make out in each slice. With a light texture that snaps when you bite and a familiar apple flavour, they appeal to anyone looking for a fruity alternative to packaged snacks. The taste strikes a balance between lightly sweet and lightly tart.

Part of the fruit chips range from Gürün/Sivas-based Tufan Organik, these apple chips stand out with their small, light pack. It holds just the right amount for a bowl to share while watching a film, at the desk or when the children come home from school. Sealing the pack tightly after opening helps the slices keep their crunch.

Serving Suggestions

  • Crumble a few slices over an oat and yoghurt bowl at breakfast for added crunch.
  • Serve them with warm cinnamon milk instead of biscuits.
  • Place a few whole slices on top of an apple cake or tart as decoration.

Weight: 80 g
