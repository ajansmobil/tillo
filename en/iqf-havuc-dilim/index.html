An Orange Touch with IQF Carrot Slices

With its sweetish taste and vivid orange colour, carrot contributes both to the flavour of a dish and to the look of the plate. Tufan Organik IQF Carrot Slices offer carrot sliced into rounds and frozen without the pieces sticking together. The large 10 kg pack has been prepared with mass catering and industrial kitchen needs in mind.

Carrot rounds come in handy in a wide range of uses, from garnishes to pilaf. They keep their firmness relatively well during cooking and, when mixed with other vegetables, brighten up the plate with their colour. With the chopping stage out of the way, the kitchen team can devote its time to the recipe itself; Gürün/Sivas-based Tufan Organik provides this convenience with large-volume packaging.

Serving Suggestions

  • Sauté with peas in butter and serve as a garnish alongside meat dishes.
  • Add to lentil and vegetable soups at the start of cooking.
  • Stir into rice pilaf together with peas and sweetcorn to make a colourful pilaf.

Pack Size: 10 kg
