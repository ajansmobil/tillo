Pumpkin Seed Oil (Aromatic): For Use Drop by Drop

Tillo Kochari Pumpkin Seed Oil (Aromatic) is a pumpkin seed oil from the aromatic oils range that stands out with its small 100 ml bottle. With a colour that shifts between dark green and brown and a deep scent, it is an intense oil. The small bottle is designed for those who want to use it sparingly and in small amounts.

In Gaziantep, pumpkin seeds are known as a snack that is roasted and salted to accompany evening conversations. Tillo Kochari has prepared the oil of the same seed in a practical bottle that can be used as a seasoning in the kitchen and as a supporting oil in personal care. It preserves its aroma better when kept away from light and heat with its cap tightly closed.

Serving Suggestions

  • Add a few drops over your morning yoghurt or curd cheese.
  • Use it as a care oil by applying a few drops around the hands and nails and massaging them in.
  • Drizzle over boiled eggs or avocado toast and finish with chilli flakes.

Pack Size: 100 ml
