Black Mulberry Concentrate: A Rich Fruit Flavor with a Thick Consistency

Black mulberry concentrate is a thick, intensely aromatic fruit product made from black mulberries. Its color is a purple so deep it is almost black, and its taste wanders between sweet and tart. Although its consistency resembles grape molasses, the fruit's tangy note is more pronounced; even a small amount adds color and flavor to the plate.

Black mulberry concentrate has its place in Tillo Kochari's fruit concentrate range thanks to uses that stretch from breakfast to dessert. It can be eaten straight from the spoon or diluted with water to make a refreshing drink. Since it is concentrated, it is best to start with a small amount in recipes and increase it to taste.

Serving Suggestions

  • A breakfast spread, mixed with tahini and spread on bread
  • Black mulberry sherbet drink, diluted with cold water and ice
  • A thick sauce drizzled over ice cream, rice pudding or yogurt
