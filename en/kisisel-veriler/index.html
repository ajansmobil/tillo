Personal Data

Your personal data are collected solely to meet legal requirements, fulfil your requests and provide you with better service. The security of your personal data is a priority for us, and we take the necessary technical and administrative measures to protect these data within the framework of all relevant legislation, in particular the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

Collected personal data are accessible only to authorised persons and are not shared with third parties without your consent. You have the right to learn for what purposes your personal data are processed, how they are stored and with whom they are shared. You may also request access to your data and ask for them to be corrected, deleted or anonymised.

If you have any questions or requests regarding your personal data, you can contact us through the communication channels specified in our Privacy Policy. The security and confidentiality of your data are of great importance to us.
