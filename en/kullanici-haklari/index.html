User Rights

Everyone has the right to request the protection of their personal data. Protecting your personal data is very important to us, and accordingly we act within the framework of the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and related legislation. Users have the right to be informed about the purposes for which their data are collected, how they are stored and with whom they are shared.

You may request that your personal data be updated, corrected, deleted or anonymised. You may also request detailed information on the purposes for which the collected data are used and on the processing procedure, and obtain information about third parties involved in these processes. You can contact us at any time to exercise your rights.

For detailed information on the protection of your personal data and your rights, please review the Privacy Policy on our website. For our users, the security of your data is our priority, and we have taken the necessary technical and administrative measures.
