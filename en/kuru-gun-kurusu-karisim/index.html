Sun-Dried Fruit Mix: Apricots, Figs and Plums Together

Bringing three classic dried fruits together in a single pack, this mix pairs the tangy sweetness of apricots, the dense, seed-studded texture of figs and the sweet-tart character of plums. Poured into a bowl, the fruits in different colours and shapes make a varied plate. It is handy for anyone who wants to try all three flavours together without buying separate packs.

This mix from Gürün/Sivas-born Tufan Organik is well suited to winter evenings with tea, to breakfast alongside cheese, or to a quick treat platter when guests arrive. Simmered together in water, the three fruits yield a rich compote in which their aromas blend. It can also be chopped and folded into cake batter.

Serving Suggestions

  • Simmer the mix with cinnamon and cloves, then chill it to make a three-fruit compote.
  • Roughly chop the fruits and mix them with walnuts and honey for a breakfast plate.
  • Arrange them around chicken roasting in the oven for a slightly sweet garnish.
