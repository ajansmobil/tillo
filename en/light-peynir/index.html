Tufan Organik Light Cheese is a low-fat cheese from the Milk and Dairy Products range, offered in 400 g – a natural food with roots in Gürün/Sivas.

Weight400 gOriginTürkiye

Simple, Light Meals with Tufan Organik Light Cheese

Light cheese is a low-fat white cheese variety for those looking for a light breakfast or snack. Its taste is mild and simple, and its texture slices easily. With this 400 g cheese, Tufan Organik adapts its product line, rooted in Gürün/Sivas, to simpler table preferences as well.

Thanks to its mild taste, light cheese pairs easily with herbs, spices and vegetables. Seasoned with thyme and olive oil, it livens up a simple breakfast plate; on wholegrain crackers or cucumber slices, it makes a practical snack. It should be stored in the refrigerator in a closed container.

Serving Suggestions

  • Season the slices with thyme, chilli flakes and a few drops of olive oil and serve at breakfast.
  • Place small pieces on cucumber slices for a quick snack.
  • Make a light cheese salad with seasonal greens and walnuts.

Weight: 400 g
